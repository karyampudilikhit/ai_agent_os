How is this different from ChatGPT / Claude? +
A chatbot gives you text. Vision AI gives you an org: a CEO that plans, Supervisors that delegate, specialists that ship finished .pptx / .docx / .xlsx files and take real actions. Plus the trust layer — verification and approval-gating — that a raw model doesn't have.
What does "approval-gated" actually mean? +
Every irreversible action — sending an email, posting to Slack, publishing a file — is queued until you tap approve. Nothing leaves your control silently. You can also whitelist specific action types you want auto-approved.
Can I use my own AI model? +
Yes. Vision AI runs on gpt-oss locally by default, and plugs into Anthropic, OpenAI, or your own Ollama endpoint. Bring your own keys — we don't mark up tokens.
What happens on day one? +
Type your business into the CEO. It designs the org. You approve. First deliverable ships within the hour — usually a research doc, a spreadsheet, or a first draft of outreach. The average founder deploys three Units on day one.
