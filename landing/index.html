Common questions

Answered.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Claude?+

A chatbot gives you text. Vision AI gives you an org: a CEO that plans, Supervisors that delegate, specialists that ship finished files and take real actions — plus a verification layer and approval gate a raw model doesn't have.

What does "approval-gated" mean?+

Every irreversible action — sending an email, creating a repo, submitting a form — is queued until you tap approve. Nothing leaves your control silently.

Is this only for founders?+

Founders are the starting wedge — they feel the pain most. But the AI-employee model is role-generic from day one, so the same product expands to any knowledge worker with more work than hands.

How do you stop it hallucinating?+

A critique-and-verify loop checks every claim against a real source and strips anything unverifiable before it reaches you. In our benchmarks that cut the fabrication rate to roughly a third of a single AI call's.
